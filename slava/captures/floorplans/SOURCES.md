## Access facts (AccessAble guide for CAGB) useful for the route model
- Main entrance (Exhibition Road): 6 deep steps with handrails, ramp alternative along Exhibition Road; double doors 210 cm.
- Rear entrance (Dalby Court): automatic door 112 cm, then steep ramp 106 cm wide + 10 steps.
- Main stairs south side + similar stairs north side, floors 0–7. Public lift floors 0–7 (**never use lifts in a quake**).
Source: https://www.accessable.co.uk/imperial-college-london/south-kensington-campus/access-guides/city-and-guilds-building
