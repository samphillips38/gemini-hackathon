# Capture protocol (module 6) — do this by 12:45

**Principle: the floor plan gives the geometry, the video gives the contents.** No SLAM. Every object Argon finds is
anchored to a node/edge of the Level 2 plan graph, so we never need metric 3D of the whole building.

## Walk 1 — primary route (2–3 min video)
Room 200 -> breakout space -> past lifts/stairs -> main entrance, Exhibition Road.
## Walk 2 — alternative route (1–2 min)
Room 200 -> Dalby Court entrance.
## Room — physics deep dive (20 s)
One furnished room **on Level 2** (breakout space, Room 203 computer room, or any L2 office/kitchen) with tall furniture/monitors AND the door in shot. Slow 360 pan. Note which room it is so it can be pinned on the plan.

## How to film (each rule makes Argon's job easier)
- Landscape, 1080p, chest height, walk slowly (~half normal speed), no sudden turns.
- **Narrate landmarks out loud** ("leaving room 200", "lifts on my right", "breakout space", "main doors"): the audio track
  lets Argon align frames to plan nodes.
- Pause 2 s at every door, junction, stairs, and at anything that could fall or shatter (vending machines, shelving,
  display cases, glass panels/doors, notice boards, freestanding signs).
- Pan left and right at each junction so corridor width is visible.
- Avoid faces, card readers and keypads. Ask before filming inside anyone's office.
